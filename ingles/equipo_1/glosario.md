# Glosario Inglés — Team 1

| Término    | Descripción |
| :--------- | :---------- |
| `disarray` | Desorden o falta de organización. Se usa para describir un lugar, situación o conjunto de cosas que están desorganizados o en caos. |
| `yell`     | Gritar o hablar con una voz muy fuerte, generalmente para llamar la atención, expresar enojo o hacerse escuchar a distancia. |
| `dare`     | Atreverse a hacer algo, especialmente algo difícil, arriesgado o que puede dar miedo. También puede significar desafiar a alguien a hacer algo. |
| `spinning` | Girando o dando vueltas. Es la forma `-ing` de `spin`, que significa girar, hacer girar algo o dar vueltas. |

# English Glossary — Team 1

| Term       | Description |
| :--------- | :---------- |
| `disarray` | A state of disorder or lack of organization. It is used to describe a place, situation, or group of things that are messy, disorganized, or chaotic. |
| `yell`     | To shout or speak in a very loud voice, usually to get someone's attention, express anger, or be heard from a distance. |
| `dare`     | To have the courage to do something, especially something difficult, risky, or frightening. It can also mean to challenge someone to do something. |
| `spinning` | Turning around or moving in circles. It is the `-ing` form of `spin`, which means to turn, make something turn, or move around in circles. |

# Example Sentences

## `disarray`

* **My room is in complete disarray.**
* **The documents were left in disarray on the desk.**
* **After the party, the house was in disarray.**

## `yell`

* **Don't yell at me.**
* **She yelled for help when she saw the accident.**
* **I had to yell because the music was very loud.**

## `dare`

* **I dare you to jump into the pool.**
* **He didn't dare to ask her the question.**
* **Would you dare to climb that mountain?**

## `spinning`

* **The wheel is spinning very fast.**
* **The children were spinning around in circles.**
* **My head was spinning after the ride.**
